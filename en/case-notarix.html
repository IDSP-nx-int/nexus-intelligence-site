NOTARIX — Notary Operations and Document Management

An independent Power Apps and Dataverse project designed to centralize notary procedures, clients, payments, roles, digital files, operational alerts, and document generation. The solution also includes structured controls to support LFPIORPI-related operational follow-up.

Quick facts

  • Type: Independent project
  • Domain: Notary operations and documentation
  • Target users: Small notary office
  • Focus: Structured data, traceability, alerts, and documents
  • Status: Functional project and technical portfolio case

1) Context and problem

Notary operations may depend on spreadsheets, paper files, separate payment controls, manual calculations, and informal follow-up. This makes it difficult to maintain a clear view of each procedure, the people involved, outstanding documents, deadlines, and payments.

2) Implemented solution

Main modules

  • Procedure management: registration and operational follow-up.
  • Client registry: people, roles, master data, and history.
  • Payments: amounts, concepts, receipts, and procedure relationships.
  • Digital case file: documents and evidence linked to each procedure.
  • Document generation: Word templates with dynamic tokens.

Delivery approach

  • Canvas app developed with Power Apps and Dataverse.
  • Structured tables, relationships, catalogs, and validations.
  • Power Automate flows for document generation and supporting processes.
  • SharePoint and OneDrive for templates, evidence, and generated files.

The objective was to reduce repeated capture, centralize files, and improve operational visibility.

3) Architecture and data structure

Data model

  • Dataverse tables for procedures, people, roles, payments, catalogs, and digital files.
  • Relationships between people, procedures, roles, payments, and documents.
  • Catalogs for statuses, deed types, legal acts, roles, and operational parameters.
  • Structured fields to reduce free-text capture and support future reporting.

Automation and documents

  • Power Automate for document generation and file handling.
  • SharePoint and OneDrive for templates, generated documents, and evidence.
  • Word templates with replaceable tokens populated from application data.
  • Operational views for status, pending items, and deadlines.

4) Key features

  • Procedure 360 view: data, people, roles, payments, documents, and status in one workflow.
  • Digital case file: documentation linked to the corresponding procedure and client.
  • Operational alerts: status indicators and deadline follow-up.
  • Person roles: buyer, seller, borrower, or other roles depending on the procedure.
  • Document generation: Word templates populated with dynamic fields.
  • Reporting-ready foundation: structured data prepared for KPI and operational reporting.

5) Scope

Included

  • Power Apps Canvas application.
  • Dataverse data model with core tables and relationships.
  • Modules for procedures, clients, payments, roles, and digital files.
  • Supporting Power Automate flows.
  • Word templates for document generation.

Future improvements

  • Power BI dashboards for procedure and deadline monitoring.
  • More formal role-based security for sensitive information.
  • Managed solutions and Dev/Test/Prod ALM.
  • Regression testing for critical rules and calculations.
