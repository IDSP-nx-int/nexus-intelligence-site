NOTARIX — Notary management with Power Apps and Dataverse

Application to centralize notary cases, clients, payments, roles, digital files, operational alerts, and document generation using Power Apps, Dataverse, Power Automate, and SharePoint.

Contact

Quick facts

  • Type: independent project
  • Domain: notary management and documentation
  • Target users: small notary office
  • Screens: multiple operational modules
  • Focus: traceability, structured capture, and document generation

1) Context / problem

Notary operations can depend on spreadsheets, scattered documents, manual calculations, separate payment tracking, and informal case follow-up. This makes it difficult to maintain a clear view of progress, documentation, people involved, and operational pending items.

2) Solution

Main modules

  • Cases: registration and follow-up of notary operations.
  • People registry: clients, roles, and master data.
  • Payments: amounts, concepts, receipts, and relationship to cases.
  • Digital file: documents, evidence, and related files.
  • Document generation: Word templates with dynamic tokens.

Delivery approach

  • Application built with Power Apps Canvas and Dataverse.
  • Data model with tables, relationships, catalogs, and business rules.
  • Power Automate flows for document generation and auxiliary processes.
  • SharePoint / OneDrive for documents, evidence, and generated files.

The goal is to reduce recapture, organize digital files, and improve process visibility.

3) Model and structure

Data

  • Dataverse as the base for cases, people, roles, payments, catalogs, and files.
  • Relationships between people, cases, roles, payments, and documents.
  • Catalogs for statuses, deed types, legal acts, roles, and operational parameters.
  • Structured fields to reduce free-text capture and improve future reporting.

Automation and documents

  • Power Automate to orchestrate document generation and file handling.
  • SharePoint / OneDrive for templates, generated documents, and digital case files.
  • Word templates with replaceable tokens from app-captured data.
  • Operational views for pending items and status follow-up.

4) Key features

  • Case 360 view: data, people, roles, payments, documents, and status in one flow.
  • Digital file: documentation linked to the case and the corresponding client.
  • Operational alerts: semaphores and follow-up signals for relevant pending items.
  • Person roles: buyer, seller, borrower, or other roles depending on the case.
  • Document generation: Word templates with dynamic fields to reduce recapture.
  • Reporting foundation: structured data ready for KPIs and later follow-up.

5) Scope

Includes

  • Power Apps Canvas application.
  • Dataverse model with main tables and relationships.
  • Modules for cases, registry, payments, roles, and files.
  • Auxiliary flows with Power Automate.
  • Word templates for document generation.

Future foundation

  • Power BI dashboards for case and operational follow-up.
  • Stronger role-based security formalization.
  • ALM with Dev/Test/Prod environments.
  • Automated tests for critical rules.

7) Next improvements

  • Formalize ALM with managed solutions, environment variables, and connection references.
  • Add regression tests for critical rules and operational calculations.
  • Build Power BI dashboards for cases, documents, timelines, and pending items.
  • Strengthen role-based security and permissions over sensitive data.
