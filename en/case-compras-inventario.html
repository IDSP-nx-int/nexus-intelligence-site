Portfolio MVP · Dataverse · Model-driven App

Procurement & Inventory MVP

A technical portfolio project developed with Dataverse and Power Apps Model-driven Apps to demonstrate procurement, requisition, purchase order, inventory, Business Process Flow, and approval patterns.

View Portfolio LinkedIn

Quick facts

  • Type: Technical portfolio MVP
  • Modules: Requisitions, Purchase Orders, Inventory, Catalogs
  • BPFs: Requisition and Purchase Order
  • Flows: Basic approvals with Power Automate
  • Status: Prototype, not a production deployment

1) Context and objective

This MVP was created as a portfolio project to practice Dataverse data modeling, model-driven app configuration, Business Process Flows, and approval automation.

Procurement and inventory were selected because they involve clear relationships between products, suppliers, projects, requisitions, purchase orders, and inventory movements.

2) Implemented solution

MVP core

  • Model-driven app organized into procurement, inventory, projects, and catalogs.
  • Dataverse model with related operational and catalog tables.
  • Requisition BPF covering capture, approval request, resolution, and closing.
  • Purchase Order BPF covering capture, approval, issuing, and follow-up.
  • Approval flows with Power Automate and Dataverse status updates.

Design approach

  • Separation of transactional tables and reusable catalogs.
  • Use of lookups, choices, and relationships to reduce free-text capture.
  • Stage-based process guidance for more consistent user execution.
  • Foundation prepared for future rules, validations, permissions, and reporting.

3) Data model and automation

Data model

  • Products, suppliers, customers, and projects as base tables.
  • Requisitions and purchase orders as transactional tables.
  • Inventory movements and balances for operational tracking.
  • Relationships designed to support forms, views, BPFs, and flows.

Automation

  • Business Process Flows for requisition and purchase order stages.
  • Power Automate approvals for approve/reject decisions.
  • Status updates in Dataverse based on approval outcomes.
  • Foundation for inventory validations and authorization rules.

4) Key features

  • Process-guided user experience: visible stages through Business Process Flows.
  • Relational structure: catalogs referenced through lookups instead of free text.
  • Basic approvals: approval results reflected in Dataverse status fields.
  • Inventory foundation: movements and balances prepared for stronger future rules.
  • Visual documentation: screenshots of tables, columns, BPFs, flows, and sitemap.

5) Scope

Included

  • Main tables for products, suppliers, customers, and projects.
  • Requisitions, purchase orders, and line items.
  • Inventory movements and balances.
  • Two Business Process Flows.
  • Basic approval flows.

Not included in this version

  • Power BI dashboards.
  • Complete role and permission matrix.
  • Formal Dev/Test/Prod ALM.
  • Load testing or production hardening.

6) Potential next improvements

  • Add role-based access for requester, approver, procurement, and warehouse users.
  • Add inventory rules such as no negative stock, movement types, and audit history.
  • Add operational dashboards for backlog, cycle time, and aging approvals.
  • Formalize ALM with managed solutions, environment variables, and connection references.
