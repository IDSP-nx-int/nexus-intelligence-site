IMPLEMENTED CASE · HUMAN RESOURCES

Attendance and employee justification analytics and automation

A solution developed within the Human Resources area of the Secretariat of Culture of Coahuila to manage attendance, requests, approvals, evidence, and operational reporting.

  • Standardized request intake.
  • Traceable approvals for managers and Human Resources.
  • Operational logs in Excel Online and SharePoint.
  • Power BI dashboards for absences, lateness, incidents, and trends.
  • Support for approximately 100 users and 450 monthly requests.

Outcome: stronger traceability, less manual follow-up, and better visibility into attendance incidents, absences, and employee justification requests.

NOTARIX · INDEPENDENT PROJECT

Notary operations, digital files, and document generation

An independent project developed with Power Apps, Dataverse, Power Automate, and SharePoint to centralize notary procedures, clients, payments, roles, documents, operational alerts, and document generation.

  • Registration and follow-up of procedures, clients, amounts, payments, and folios.
  • Dataverse tables, relationships, catalogs, forms, and validations.
  • Operational alerts and status indicators.
  • Digital files with evidence linked to each procedure.
  • Word templates with dynamic tokens for standardized documents.

Outcome: structured information, centralized operations, stronger traceability, and less reliance on scattered controls.
